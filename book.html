The CLEAR Process

A simple way to read every shot

The heart of the book: a five-step routine you can run before every shot around the green, so you commit with confidence instead of guessing.

C

Check the lie

Read the lie first. It decides what's actually possible, and stops you attempting a shot the lie won't allow.

L

Landing spot & shot choice

Pick the exact spot you want the ball to land, then choose the shot that gets it there.

E

Equipment

Select the right club for the shot you've chosen, not just the one you always reach for.

A

Action

Rehearse with purpose. Practice swings that feel the real shot, not vague waggles.

R

Release

Clear your mind, trust the process and let the shot go. No second-guessing over the ball.
