About

A BIT ABOUT ME

I'm Andy Plester, a PGA Professional with over 22 years of coaching experience, specialising in short game performance and on-course confidence.

Most golfers practice mechanics.

Few know how to perform when it matters.

My coaching focuses on the shots inside 100 yards, where rounds are won or lost, and the confidence required to execute them under pressure.

No gimmicks.
No fake motivation.
Just structured skill development and real performance clarity.

If you want your short game and your confidence to finally work together, you're in the right place.

  • ⛳ Proven Short Game Systems Structured practice inside 100 yards: distance control, trajectory awareness, bunker clarity and scoring skill that actually transfers to the course.
  • 🎯 From Range to Course Stop practising well and scoring poorly. Build routines and decision-making systems that hold up under pressure.
  • 🧠 Confidence Under Pressure Calm decisions. Clear commitment. Emotional control when it matters most.
