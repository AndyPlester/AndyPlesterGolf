About

THE REAL SIDE OF
COACHING

I'm Andy Plester — a PGA Coach passionate about helping golfers of all levels play with confidence.

I've spent years coaching players to trust their swing, calm their nerves and take their best game from the range to the course. No flashy edits or fake motivation. Just what actually works.

My goal is simple: to help you build confidence that lasts every round. If you've ever wondered how to play calm, focused and free from doubt — stick around.

  • ⛳ Proven drills On-course confidence — the same drills I use with my students every week.
  • 🎯 Range to course Stop playing great on the range and tense on the course. Bring your best swing when it counts.
  • 📹 No filter, no script Clear, easy-to-understand advice. Professional, intuitive and relatable.
